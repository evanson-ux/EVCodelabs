# Growth Engine Builder

# PROJECT: EVCodelabs Digital Growth Website

## 1. Project objective

Build a premium, professional, responsive landing page for EVCodelabs, a technology and digital growth company that helps businesses establish, improve, automate, and grow their online presence.

The website must explain what the company does, help potential customers identify the services they need, establish credibility through genuine case studies, and generate inquiries through WhatsApp and email.

The target markets are Kenya and international clients.

Do not build a generic technology agency template. Build a cohesive brand experience that communicates trust, simplicity, practicality, professionalism, confidence, and business growth.

## 2. Brand positioning

Company: EVCodelabs

Brand character: Digital Growth Partner.

Positioning: A technology and digital growth company that builds, improves, and scales businesses online.

Mission: To help businesses grow online through simple, practical, and result-driven digital systems.

Vision: To be Africa's most trusted partner for building and growing digital businesses.

Brand values:

* Trust: credibility and transparency.
* Simplicity: making technology understandable and useful.
* Excellence: delivering thoughtful, high-quality solutions.

Brand personality:

* Trustworthy
* Simple
* Practical
* Professional
* Growth-oriented

Communication tone:

* Clear
* Practical
* Professional
* Empowering
* Confident

Core message: Technology should simplify business, not complicate it.

## 3. Target audience

Primary audience:

* Small businesses
* Startups
* Service-based businesses
* Personal brands

Secondary audience:

* E-commerce businesses
* Agencies
* Established organizations requiring digital transformation

The website must communicate to both growing small businesses and established organizations. Avoid language that makes EVCodelabs appear limited to one industry or only to website development.

## 4. Technology and implementation

Use Lovable's supported project stack, preferably React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and reusable components unless the project environment requires another approach.

Build a clean, maintainable component structure. Keep content, shared components, styling tokens, and page sections logically organized.

Do not add a database, authentication, payments, or a backend unless a real requirement is established. This is initially a marketing and lead-generation website.

Do not introduce unnecessary dependencies.

## 5. Visual identity

Use these working brand colors:

* Royal blue: #243F8F
* Sage green: #9DC183
* Charcoal: #292D32
* Alabaster: #F8F7F2

Use a clean geometric sans-serif font. Manrope is the proposed primary font, subject to confirmation against the official logo and brand assets.

Use only these font weights:

* 400 regular
* 500 medium
* 600 semi-bold
* 700 bold

Typography should feel modern, structured, professional, approachable, and highly readable.

Proposed typography:

* H1: approximately 52–60px on desktop and 36–42px on mobile.
* H2: approximately 36–40px on desktop and 28–32px on mobile.
* H3: approximately 22–24px on desktop and 20–22px on mobile.
* Body: 16–18px on desktop and at least 16px on mobile.
* Captions: 13–14px, subject to readability testing.
* Buttons: 15–16px, medium or semi-bold.

Use fluid responsive typography, readable line heights, consistent spacing, accessible contrast, and clear heading hierarchy.

Most sections should use light backgrounds. Use charcoal for the Our Approach feature section. Use royal blue for important actions and sage green for restrained growth-related accents.

Avoid decorative fonts, excessive gradients, neon effects, excessive animations, clutter, and unnecessary visual effects.

## 6. Homepage content and section order

Build exactly these eight sections in this order:

### A. Navigation

* EVCodelabs logo or a temporary text-based logo until the official logo is provided.
* Services
* Our Approach
* Case Studies
* Contact
* Primary CTA: Discuss a Project

Navigation items must lead to the correct homepage sections. Provide an accessible mobile navigation menu.

### B. Hero

Eyebrow:
YOUR DIGITAL GROWTH PARTNER

H1:
Turn your digital potential into business growth.

Supporting copy:
We help businesses build a strong online presence, reach more customers, automate everyday work, and grow with practical digital solutions tailored to their goals.

Primary CTA:
Discuss a Project

Secondary CTA:
Explore Our Services

The hero should immediately communicate business value. Use a clean composition and restrained visual support. Do not invent company statistics, awards, or customer logos.

### C. Services

Heading:
Everything you need to move your business forward.

Supporting copy:
Whether you're starting out, improving an existing business, or preparing to scale, we help you identify and implement the digital solutions that make sense for your goals.

Create four visually consistent service categories:

BUILD
Create the digital foundations your business needs to stand out and operate online.

Services:
Branding, website design and development, e-commerce, web and mobile applications, custom software, and UI/UX design.

GROW
Help more of the right people discover your business and take action.

Services:
SEO, local SEO, Google Business Profile, social media management, content, paid advertising, and lead generation.

AUTOMATE
Reduce repetitive tasks and make everyday business processes more efficient.

Services:
Workflow automation, CRM systems, WhatsApp and email automation, booking systems, notifications, and reporting.

SCALE
Improve your digital systems as your business, customers, and needs grow.

Services:
AI integrations, business analytics, system integrations, performance optimization, advanced software, and ongoing technical support.

Include a CTA:
Not sure what your business needs? Let's work it out together.

### D. Our Approach

Use a charcoal background with accessible text contrast and sage-green accents.

Heading:
Technology should simplify business, not complicate it.

Supporting copy:
Every business has different goals, challenges, and opportunities. We start by understanding yours, then identify the right digital solutions to help you move forward.

Present three steps:

1. Understand your business.
2. Find the right solution.
3. Build and improve.

Keep the design professional and uncluttered.

### E. Case Studies

Heading:
Real challenges. Practical solutions. Meaningful progress.

Supporting copy:
Explore how we approach business challenges, turn ideas into digital solutions, and improve the way businesses work online.

Create a reusable case-study card design that can accommodate:

* Project or client name, when disclosure is permitted.
* The challenge.
* The goal.
* The solution.
* Technologies used, when relevant.
* Verified outcomes, where available.
* A link to further details.

IMPORTANT: Do not fabricate clients, projects, testimonials, logos, revenue, performance metrics, or outcomes. Use only information supplied by the business owner. If no approved case studies are available yet, create a clearly labelled empty state or neutral placeholder that can be populated later. Do not present fictional examples as real client work.

### F. How We Work

Heading:
From your idea to a practical solution.

Present four steps:

1. Let's Talk — understand the business, goals, and challenges.
2. Plan the Solution — agree on scope, priorities, timeline, and cost.
3. Build and Deliver — create and review the agreed solution.
4. Improve and Grow — offer ongoing improvements and support where agreed.

Use a clear, easy-to-scan layout.

### G. FAQs

Include these questions and answers:

What services does EVCodelabs provide?
We help businesses build their online presence, attract customers, improve processes, and grow using services such as website development, SEO, branding, software development, automation, AI integration, and digital marketing.

Who do you work with?
We work with startups, small businesses, service providers, personal brands, e-commerce businesses, and established organizations.

Do I need to know exactly what service I need?
No. Tell us about your goals or challenges, and we can discuss which solutions may be suitable for your business.

Can you work with businesses outside Kenya?
Yes. EVCodelabs aims to serve businesses in Kenya and international markets. Delivery arrangements depend on the project and service required.

How much do your services cost?
Pricing depends on the work involved, required features, and project scope. Contact us to discuss your needs and receive a proposal.

Do you provide support after a project is completed?
Support and maintenance can be discussed and included in the project agreement where required.

The FAQ interaction must be accessible by keyboard and usable on mobile.

### H. Contact and Footer

Heading:
Ready to move your business forward?

Supporting copy:
Whether you need to establish your online presence, reach more customers, improve your systems, or explore a new idea, let's talk about what your business needs.

Primary CTA:
Start a Conversation

Include WhatsApp and email contact options. Use actual contact information provided by the owner. Do not invent addresses or social accounts.

Footer should contain the EVCodelabs identity, useful navigation, contact options, and a copyright notice with the current year generated dynamically.

Do not add unverified legal entity wording or claims.

## 7. Functionality

* Navigation scrolls to the correct sections.
* Mobile menu opens, closes, and supports keyboard navigation.
* CTA buttons work.
* WhatsApp opens the correct conversation with a relevant prefilled message.
* Email links open a prepared inquiry.
* Case-study links work when destinations exist.
* FAQs work accessibly.
* Hover and focus states are consistent.
* Respect reduced-motion preferences.
* No dead buttons, fabricated form submissions, or misleading success messages.

Do not build a fake contact form that claims to submit data without a working delivery mechanism.

## 8. SEO requirements

The primary market is Kenya, with international reach.

Proposed homepage SEO title:
Digital Growth & IT Services in Kenya | EVCodelabs

Proposed meta description:
EVCodelabs helps businesses in Kenya and beyond grow online with website development, SEO, digital marketing, automation, AI, and custom software solutions.

H1:
Turn your digital potential into business growth.

Implement:

* A unique title and meta description.
* A canonical URL based on the final production domain.
* Semantic HTML and logical heading hierarchy.
* Descriptive internal links.
* Useful image alt text.
* Optimized image sizes and formats.
* Sitemap and robots.txt appropriate to the deployment.
* Appropriate structured data only when the details are accurate.
* Good mobile usability and page performance.
* Google Search Console readiness.

Avoid keyword stuffing, hidden text, fake local addresses, and claims of guaranteed search rankings.

Treat proposed keywords as hypotheses that must be validated:

* digital solutions company in Kenya
* IT services for businesses in Kenya
* website development company in Kenya
* SEO services in Kenya
* business automation services
* e-commerce website development
* web development services
* digital marketing services
* custom software development

Do not force every keyword into the homepage. Plan for dedicated service pages in a later phase.

## 9. Accessibility and responsive design

* Support mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop.
* Use semantic HTML.
* Ensure readable text and adequate color contrast.
* Provide visible keyboard focus indicators.
* Make interactive elements keyboard accessible.
* Provide meaningful alternative text for informative images.
* Ensure buttons and links have descriptive accessible names.
* Avoid horizontal overflow.
* Keep animations subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 10. Quality assurance

Before declaring the website complete:

* Run the project's available checks.
* Fix TypeScript, build, and runtime errors.
* Check for broken links and missing assets.
* Test all contact actions.
* Inspect desktop and mobile layouts.
* Verify metadata and indexing controls.
* Check loading performance and accessibility.
* Clearly report anything that remains unfinished.

## 11. Build process

Work in phases. Start by reviewing this brief and proposing the project structure and implementation approach. Do not immediately generate the entire site in one uncontrolled pass.

First establish the design system and shared components. Then build the homepage sections in the specified order. Test and refine after each phase.

Preserve approved brand decisions and content throughout the project. Ask only when an essential business detail is missing. Never invent evidence or claim that a feature works without testing it.

The final result should feel like a coherent, premium EVCodelabs brand experience: clear, practical, professional, approachable, and focused on business growth.
